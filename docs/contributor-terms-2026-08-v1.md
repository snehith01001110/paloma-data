# Paloma Establishment Data Contribution Terms — 2026-08-v1

Status: Owner-approved for development on 2026-08-20; production legal review remains required

By submitting establishment information, the contributor confirms that:

1. The information is based on the contributor's firsthand observation or, for a merchant
   contribution, the contributor is authorized to provide it for the establishment.
2. The contributor will not copy information from a source whose terms prohibit storage,
   redistribution, analysis, or derivative use.
3. To the extent the contribution is protectable, the contributor grants Paloma a non-exclusive,
   worldwide, royalty-free, transferable, sublicensable, perpetual license to store, normalize,
   combine, verify, modify, publish, and redistribute the contribution as part of Paloma's
   establishment directory and related data products.
4. Paloma may retain the submitted value, its provenance, and review history after the displayed
   establishment record changes or the contributor's account is closed, subject to applicable law.
5. Paloma may accept, reject, qualify, or later supersede a contribution. Submission does not
   guarantee publication.
6. The contributor will not submit personal data, confidential business information, reviews,
   photographs, menu text, or other protected content through a factual field-correction form.
7. Paloma may contact an establishment representative to verify a merchant claim or submitted
   fact.

These terms cover factual establishment fields such as regular hours, contact information,
location, operating status, price ranges, and objectively defined amenities. Separate terms are
required for photographs, prose descriptions, reviews, menus, or other creative content.

This version may be activated for development and internal release-governance testing. Before
Paloma enables public contributions in production, the owner must complete legal review and either
reapprove this exact version for production or publish and activate a superseding version.
